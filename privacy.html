Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how D4rk Rebel Games (“D4rk Rebel Games,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with this website (d4rkrebel.com) and the video games and applications we publish on mobile, desktop, and console platforms (together, the “Services”).

1. Who we are

D4rk Rebel Games is an independent video game developer and publisher. We are the entity responsible for the information described in this policy.

2. Information we collect

This website. This website does not use cookies, advertising trackers, or analytics, and does not ask you to create an account or submit personal information. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record standard technical server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and the date and time of a request) for security and reliability purposes. These logs are not used to identify you.

Our games. Depending on the game and platform, we may collect limited information needed to operate the game, such as:

  • Device and technical data (device model, operating system version, language, crash and performance reports);
  • Gameplay data (progress, settings, and in-game activity) needed to save your game and improve balance and stability;
  • Purchase records for in-app purchases, which are processed by the platform store (for example, the Apple App Store or Google Play). We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Where a game collects any information beyond what is described here, or uses third-party services such as advertising or analytics SDKs, that game’s store listing and in-game privacy notice will describe what is collected and why, and this policy will be updated accordingly.

Game-specific policies. The Old King’s Tower collects anonymous usage data for analytics; its own privacy policy describes what is collected and how to turn it off.

3. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and fix the Services;
  • To save game progress and deliver purchased content;
  • To understand and improve performance and gameplay;
  • To protect the security and integrity of the Services and prevent cheating or fraud;
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising unless a specific game clearly discloses that and, where required, asks for your consent.

4. How we share information

We share information only with:

  • Service providers that help us operate the Services (such as hosting and crash reporting), who may use the information only on our behalf;
  • Platform operators (such as Apple, Google, and console manufacturers) as needed to deliver games and process purchases, subject to their own privacy policies;
  • Authorities, when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security;
  • A successor entity in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

5. Data retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes described above, and then delete or anonymize it. Server logs are retained for a limited period by our hosting provider.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can also manage device-level permissions (such as tracking or notifications) in your device settings, and you can delete a game’s local data by uninstalling it. Where a game supports accounts, it will provide a way to request account and data deletion.

7. Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country) without verifiable parental consent. If we learn that we have collected such information without consent, we will delete it. Any game directed at children will comply with applicable children’s privacy laws and platform family policies.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and to limit what we collect in the first place.

9. International transfers

Information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date above, and for material changes we will provide additional notice where appropriate.

11. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, or want to exercise your privacy rights (including requesting access to or deletion of your data), email us at support@d4rkrebel.com.
