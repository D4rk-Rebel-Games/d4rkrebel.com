The Old King’s Tower

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game The Old King’s Tower (the “Game”) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The Game is made by D4rk Rebel Games (“we,” “us,” or “our”). Our studio Privacy Policy covers this website and our other games; where the two differ for this Game, this policy applies.

In short

  • The Game has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.
  • Your game profile (settings and progress) is stored on your device.
  • By default, the Game sends anonymous usage data about how games are played, so we can balance and improve it. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

1. Information the Game collects

Game profile data. The Game keeps a profile on your device so it can remember your progress and preferences. It includes your settings (sound, music, haptics, look, and data sharing), your chosen map and difficulty, saved runs, scores and records, in-game currency (souls), items unlocked in the in-game store, badges, and Edicts. The profile also holds a randomly generated player ID and the date the Game was first opened. The player ID is not tied to your name, Apple ID, or device identifiers.

Usage and analytics data. While data sharing is on, the Game sends information about how it is played, such as:

  • When the Game is opened, and when runs and nights start and end;
  • How a run went: the map and difficulty, how far it got, score, which enemies got through, which towers were built and how they performed, gold earned and spent, research chosen, powers used, and time played;
  • Store unlocks and tutorial steps completed;
  • Performance information, such as frame times;
  • Some profile details: your settings, souls, store unlocks, and how many days since you first opened the Game.

Each event includes the random player ID, the Game’s version, your device model, operating system and version, and screen shape (phone or tablet).

Technical data received by our analytics provider. When the Game sends data, our analytics provider receives your device’s IP address as part of the internet connection and may use it to estimate an approximate location, such as country or city.

What the Game does not collect. The Game does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, precise location, or the advertising identifier (IDFA). It does not use cookies or advertising SDKs, and it does not offer real-money purchases.

2. How we use information

  • To save your progress and settings and run the Game;
  • To understand how the Game is played, such as how far runs get and which nights are too hard or too easy, so we can balance and improve it;
  • To find and fix bugs and performance problems;
  • To protect the Game and comply with legal obligations.

We do not use this information to identify you, to build advertising profiles, or to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

3. How information is shared

We use PostHog to receive and analyze usage data. PostHog processes it on our behalf as our service provider, on servers in the United States. Apart from PostHog, we share information only when required by law, to protect rights, safety, and security, or with a successor entity in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets. We do not sell your information.

4. Your choices

  • Turn off data sharing. Open Settings in the Game and switch off Share play data. The Game then stops sending usage data and discards any data it has not yet sent.
  • Delete your game profile. Deleting the Game from your device removes your profile, saved runs, and player ID. If you install it again, it starts with a new, unrelated player ID.

5. Data retention

Your game profile stays on your device until you delete the Game. Usage data that has not yet been sent is kept on your device for up to two weeks and then discarded. Usage data we have received is kept only as long as it is useful for improving the Game, and is then deleted.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict its processing. Because the usage data we receive is linked only to a random player ID, and not to your name or contact details, we may not be able to find the data that relates to you. If you want to make a request, email us and we will do our best to help.

7. Children’s privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Usage data is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). We use reasonable measures to protect information, and we limit what we collect in the first place. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. International transfers

Usage data is processed in the United States, which may be outside the country where you live. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game changes. When we do, we will update the effective date above, and for material changes we will give additional notice where appropriate, for example in the Game or its release notes.

11. Contact us

Questions or requests about this policy can be sent to support@d4rkrebel.com with the subject line “Privacy Request.”
